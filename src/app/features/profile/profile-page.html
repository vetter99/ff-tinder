How suggestions are made

  • When on, the app also learns how you value whole positions. If you keep picking RBs over WRs, every RB gets a small boost and every WR a small penalty, including players you've never seen in a matchup. That learns faster, but it can suggest "any RB for any WR" trades. When off, each answer only changes the two players you compared.
  • When on, Targets, Trade Ideas and the higher/lower lists only include players who have appeared in at least one of your matchups (for a trade, at least one of the two players). That way every suggestion traces back to an answer you gave about that player. When off, suggestions can also come from your position lean or from small knock-on effects.
